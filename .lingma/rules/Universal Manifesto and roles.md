## 📜 1. UNIVERSAL RULES (APPLIES TO ALL AGENTS)
- **Architecture:** Pure Vanilla JS, Firebase v10, strict Modularity, and `window.AppModules` structure. NO React, NO Vue.
- **Modification Protocol:** Do not rewrite entire files. Use diff-like targeted changes. Always provide a 3-step plan before execution.
- **Data & State:** State management is mutation-free (`__index` must be preserved). `/ui` (View) and `/services` (Data) are strictly separated.
- **Commenting:** All logic blocks must include `// [WHY]` and `// [HOW]` comments.
